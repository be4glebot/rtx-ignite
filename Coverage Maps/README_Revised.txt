KYLE FIELD v9 — REVISED COVERAGE VIEWER
Open Kyle_Field_v9_Coverage_Viewer.html in a browser. It is fully self-contained.
RF and day-camera layers are ON by default. Use checkboxes to separate them, the altitude selector to switch radar shadow layers, and the view buttons to inspect the campus or stadium. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Save visible map as PNG exports the current map.
Building shadows are sensor line-of-sight obstructions. They use the supplied campus footprints, modeled building heights and CAD stadium. Radar height is 28.1 m above modeled ground including the garage and 4.2 m mast. Low sensor placement relative to neighboring structures is included; this does not assume arbitrarily tall towers.
The 15 m / 30 m radar layers are conservative geometric reconstructions, not the original simulation coverage raster. Nominal small-target range is 1.1 km. Heights may be estimated; terrain, trees, radar elevation masks and propagation effects are not included. RF/camera rings are nominal envelopes, not obstruction-corrected coverage guarantees. PTZ cameras cannot observe their full envelope simultaneously.
No accuracy, alarm-rate or coverage-percentage panels are shown.
