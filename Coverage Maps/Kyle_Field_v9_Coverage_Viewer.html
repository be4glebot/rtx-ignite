How blind spots are reduced

Garage sites give radars more open views. The second radar covers some of the first radar’s blocked directions. Corner cameras support bowl verification; RF adds observations from transmitting drones. Remaining gaps stay visible to the operator.

Geometry and assumptions

The uploaded GeoJSON supplies campus footprints; the v9 demo supplies their local positions and modeled heights plus the FreeCAD stadium mesh. Garage radars sit 28.1 m above model ground: approximately 23.9 m garage height plus 4.2 m mast. Edge cameras are at 39 m in the demo.

Radar shading uses a conservative 5 m obstacle-height raster and a 10 m target grid. Building heights are often estimates. Terrain is flat; trees, weather, multipath and radar elevation masks are not modeled. The original simulation’s percentage results are not reproduced here.

Camera circles are pointing envelopes, not simultaneous visibility. Buildings and image resolution limit usable camera range. RF circles do not guarantee reception or detection of radio-silent drones.
