    Event log

      Simulated flights and assumed sensor performance. Model calls were produced by the trained model on these exact runs and are replayed here. A call must persist 2 s to step up in concern and 8 s to step down. Status is what the operator gets: a threat call only becomes an ALARM once it is a confirmed drone (camera, rotor signature or drone radio) and urgent (over the bowl, within 600 m, or under 2 minutes away); until then it shows as confirming. Six sites: four stadium corner roofs (camera + RF sensor) and the Central Campus and West Campus garage roofs (radar + camera + RF sensor). The six cameras are shared: each looks at one drone at a time, assigned by urgency, and only where it has a clear line of sight; none of these cases were used in training. "New type" marks scenario types the model never saw at all. Line of sight for every sensor uses our FreeCAD stadium model and campus buildings from OpenStreetMap (most heights estimated from floor counts; trees not included).
